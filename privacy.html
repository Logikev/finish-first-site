PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Finish First is a local-first productivity application designed to help users focus on one active project, capture ideas, plan milestones, and track progress.

Information stored by Finish First

Finish First stores the project information you enter directly on your device.

This may include:

  • Projects
  • Milestones
  • Daily Focus tasks
  • Weekly Sprint tasks
  • Parked ideas
  • Completion history
  • Notes and planning information
  • App preferences

The current beta version uses a local SQLite database managed by Drift.

Accounts

Finish First Beta does not require you to create an account.

There is currently no Finish First cloud account, remote user database, or login system.

Data collection

Finish First Beta does not intentionally collect or transmit your project data to Finish First servers.

The current beta does not include:

  • Advertising
  • Behavioral tracking
  • Third-party analytics
  • Location tracking
  • Contact-list access
  • Cloud synchronization
  • Marketing trackers

Backups

Finish First includes an optional backup feature.

When you choose Export Backup, the app creates a backup containing your Finish First data. You decide where that backup is saved or shared using your device's operating-system sharing features.

Finish First does not automatically upload your backups.

If you import a backup, the selected backup is used to restore Finish First data on your device.

Beta feedback and support

If you voluntarily send feedback, support requests, screenshots, crash information, or other information, that information may be used to:

  • Respond to your request
  • Diagnose problems
  • Improve Finish First
  • Evaluate the beta experience

Apple TestFlight

Beta versions of Finish First may be distributed using Apple TestFlight.

When you use TestFlight, Apple may process technical, diagnostic, account, and feedback information according to Apple's own privacy policies and TestFlight practices.

Data deletion

Because Finish First Beta stores its primary data locally, you can remove your Finish First data by:

  • Deleting individual records inside the app
  • Using the app's reset feature
  • Deleting the application and its local data

Exported backup files must be deleted separately from wherever you chose to save them.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated as Finish First evolves.

If future versions introduce features such as cloud synchronization or accounts, this policy will be updated to reflect those changes.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be sent through the Finish First Support page.
