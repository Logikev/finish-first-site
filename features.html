FOCUS • COMMIT • FINISH

A project planner designed to help you finish.

Finish First deliberately keeps your attention on one active project while giving every new idea a safe place to wait.

01 — DASHBOARD

See what matters now.

The Dashboard keeps your active project, finish definition, milestone progress, next action and parked ideas visible without overwhelming you with everything you might do someday.

  • One active project
  • Milestone progress
  • Visible finish definition
  • Tiny next action
02 — ACTIVE PROJECT

Define what finished means.

Instead of simply adding more tasks, Finish First asks you to define a real finish line and maintain one visible next action.

  • Finish definition
  • Target date
  • Milestones
  • Next action
03 — PROJECT QUEUE

Decide what deserves your attention next.

Score potential projects by priority, impact and effort, but keep only one project active at a time.

  • Priority
  • Impact
  • Effort
  • Focus score
04 — IDEA PARKING LOT

Capture ideas without chasing them.

New ideas don't have to disappear, but they also don't have to derail your current project.

  • Capture immediately
  • Set review dates
  • Score urgency and value
  • Promote an idea to the Project Queue
05 — DAILY FOCUS

Choose only three things today.

Finish First deliberately limits your Daily Focus to three must-do tasks. The objective isn't to manage an infinite list. It's to make meaningful progress.

  • Daily Top 3
  • Time blocks
  • Completion tracking
  • Small next actions
06 — WEEKLY SPRINT

Turn the finish line into this week's work.

Plan a small weekly outcome and connect each task back to the milestone you're trying to finish.

  • Weekly outcome
  • Milestone-linked tasks
  • Energy awareness
  • Simple weekly review

One project. One finish line.
